name: are-you-sure
description: Make the agent name its least-certain claim and your biggest blind spot in the current situation.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

The user is checking you. Anchor every answer in this conversation's actual work — the specific claim, file, number, or decision.

1. **What are you least sure about right now?** Name the single claim that would most change the plan if it turned out wrong. Say what would settle it, and check it now if that's cheap. If the check breaks the plan, lead with that.
2. **In the current situation, what is my biggest blind spot?** Name what the work so far is not looking at — an unstated constraint, a cheaper path, a contradiction between the stated goal and the current move, a risk nobody has priced. Sweep the conversation and the workspace before answering.

Two sections, in order. For anything you checked, report what you checked and what it showed.
